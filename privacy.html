Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Short version: Pip doesn't collect anything about you. No accounts, no tracking, no ads, no analytics.

The Pip app

Pip runs entirely on your computer. It doesn't need an internet connection and doesn't send any data anywhere. To know where your windows are (so he can walk on them), Pip looks at the size and position of open windows. It never reads what's inside them, and none of it leaves your computer.

Your settings (like his colour, hat and your pet's name) are saved in a small file on your own computer, in your user folder. If you uninstall Pip, you can delete that file too.

If Pip ever has a problem, it may write a crash log to that same folder. It stays on your computer unless you choose to send it to us.

Children

Pip is made to be enjoyed by all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies or analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address in the normal way any web request does.

Buying Pip

Purchases are handled by our payment provider, which acts as the seller of record and processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details so we can deliver Pip and help if something goes wrong. We never see your card number.

Emailing us

If you email support@pipslittlelife.com, we'll use your email only to reply to you.
