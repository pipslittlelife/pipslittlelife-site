Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Short version: Pip doesn't collect anything about you. No accounts, no tracking, no ads, no analytics.

The Pip app

Pip runs entirely on your computer. It doesn't need an internet connection and never sends anything anywhere. To walk on your windows and react to what you're doing, Pip notices a few things, all on your PC, in the moment:

  • Your windows: where open windows are and how big, the name of the program each belongs to, and the title of the window in front (only to spot a few things, like a video site, so Pip can get the popcorn).
  • Sound: Pip listens to the sound your PC is playing to dance in time and to notice when you're on a call. It's analysed on the spot and never recorded. Pip never uses your microphone.
  • Typing and your mouse: whether you're typing or away, and where your mouse pointer and text cursor are (so Pip can step out of the way). Never which keys you press.
  • Your battery: on a laptop, the battery level and whether it's plugged in (so Pip can take a little charging nap when it's running low).

Pip never reads your documents, web pages or messages, and none of this is saved or leaves your computer.

Your settings (like Pip's colour, hat, your pet's name and, if you add it, your birthday's month and day) are saved on your own computer in two small folders, %LOCALAPPDATA%\tdb-pip and %LOCALAPPDATA%\tdb-shared. Uninstalling Pip keeps them (so a reinstall remembers Pip); delete those folders too if you'd like everything gone.

If Pip ever has a problem, it may write a crash log to that same folder. It stays on your computer unless you choose to send it to us.

Children

Pip is made to be enjoyed by all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies or analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address in the normal way any web request does.
