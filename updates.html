How to get the newest Pip

Bought Pip on this website? Open the download page from your purchase (the page you landed on after paying; we suggested bookmarking it) and download Pip again. Run the installer: it updates Pip in place and keeps Pip's look, pet and everything Pip has built. Lost the link? Email support@pipslittlelife.com with your receipt and we'll send it.

Got Pip from the Microsoft Store or Steam? Updates arrive on their own.

Pip never goes online to check for updates. That's why Pip sends you here instead.
