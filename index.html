Questions

Will he slow down my computer?

No. Pip is tiny and naps when you're away. He also hides automatically during full-screen games and videos.

Is it kid-friendly?

Yes. Pip is gentle and silly, with no ads, no chat, no accounts and no internet connection needed. He doesn't collect any personal information.

Can I choose what he does on his own?

Yes. In Settings you can turn things on or off (rides, flying, snacks…), set how chatty he is, and turn sound off.

How do I hide or close him?

Press Ctrl + Alt + P to hide him and bring him back. To close him, right-click him and choose "Say bye to Pip".
