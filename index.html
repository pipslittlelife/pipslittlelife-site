Questions

What's a desktop pet?

A little character who lives on your computer screen, on top of your windows and taskbar, while you work, study or play. Some people call it a desktop buddy or desktop companion. Pip is a cozy one for Windows 10 and 11: no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and Pip works offline.

Do I have to look after Pip?

Not at all. Pip is totally hands-off: there's nothing to feed, clean or keep up with, and no guilt if you don't check in for days. Pip just gets on with a happy little life. Right-click whenever you'd like to join in.

Will Pip slow down my computer?

No. Pip is tiny and naps when you're away, and hides automatically during full-screen games and videos.

Is Pip kid-friendly?

Yes. Pip is gentle and silly, with no ads, no chat, no accounts and no internet connection needed, and collects no personal information. There's also a Kid mode (chosen on first run, or any time in Settings): the same Pip, just without the grown-up jokes about offices and coffee.

Can I choose what Pip does?

Yes. In Settings you can turn things on or off (rides, flying, snacks…), set how chatty Pip is, and turn sound off.

How do I hide or close Pip?

Press Ctrl + Alt + P to hide Pip and bring Pip back. To close, right-click Pip and choose "Say bye to Pip".
